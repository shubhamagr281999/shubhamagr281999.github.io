2021

Autonomous drone landing, DRDO challenge

Detect a landing marker from the air, plan a descent onto it, and touch down. A competition sprint, which is its own kind of engineering constraint.

  • Perception
  • Motion Planning

My part Team project. My own contributions were largely rewritten during the sprint, so the pipeline at the final commit is mostly a teammate's work. I am listing it for the experience rather than claiming the code.

Repository

Problem

The challenge asked for an autonomous quadrotor that could locate a marked landing site and land on it without a human in the loop.

Approach

A vision front end to find the marker, an estimator to hold the pose, and a guidance law bringing the aircraft down along an approach profile, all inside a simulated environment.

Outcome

The team produced a working entry. The strongest ideas in the final repository, including the penalty cascade and the trick for handling invalid regions, are a teammate's and I am not going to present them as mine.
