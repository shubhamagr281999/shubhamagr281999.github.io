Recursive Bayesian localisation, from scratch

Every state estimator I have written since is this loop with more machinery bolted on. Building the smallest version by hand is what made the rest legible.

  • Perception

My part Solo.

Repository

Problem

A robot in a corridor knows what a door looks like but not where it is. Its belief about its own position has to survive ambiguous observations and grow less certain every time it moves.

Approach

A discrete belief over positions, multiplied by an observation likelihood at each sense step and convolved with a motion kernel at each move step, normalised and plotted so the distribution is visible at every iteration.

Outcome

It does what the textbook says: sensing sharpens the belief into peaks at each door, moving smears it out again, and repeated observations collapse it onto the true position. There is no benchmark here and it is not trying to be one.
