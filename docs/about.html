About me

I am a robotics engineer at Miko in Mumbai. Right now I build the infrastructure that makes robot learning work: the curation tool that turns raw teleoperation into a training set worth using, the harness that runs a policy in simulation or on real hardware through the same code path, and the model analysis that tells the team which engineering constraints are real.

Before that I spent three years on a production autonomy stack for an indoor mobile robot, and I worked across all of it. I wrote the sysfs GPIO driver and the PID drive system at the bottom, the shared-memory middleware in the middle, and the TSDF mapper, the D* Lite planner and the local planner on top. I also built the person-following capability, with the detector running on the robot's on-board NPU, which is the project I point at when someone asks whether I have actually deployed a learned model on hardware.

I studied mechanical engineering at IIT Bombay on the five-year dual degree. My Master's thesis was on modelling and controlling a soft continuum robot, which meant casting silicone segments, building the robot, and then discovering that the actuator could not deliver the quantity my controller was written in. Changing the control interface rather than fighting the hardware is still the piece of engineering I am most pleased with.

The through-line is that I like being responsible for the whole path from the algorithm down to the thing that moves. I am looking for robot learning and embodied-AI work where the model and the robot are the same problem.

Interested in robot learning and VLA models, simulation and sim-to-real, motion planning, state estimation, embedded systems, the tooling that makes all of it repeatable.
